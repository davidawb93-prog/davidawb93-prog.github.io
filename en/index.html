05 · Before we start

A few practical questions.

What to know before getting in touch.

Which languages do you translate between?

I translate from English into Norwegian Bokmål. Norwegian is my native language, and I also offer editing, proofreading and linguistic quality assurance for Norwegian content.

How does localisation differ from translation?

Translation conveys meaning in another language. Localisation also adapts content to a market: its tone, terminology, formats and readers' expectations. I combine both when adapting websites, product content and campaigns for Norway.

Can you review machine-translated or AI-generated content?

Yes. I offer machine translation post-editing (MTPE) and linguistic quality assurance, and have experience evaluating AI-generated responses. We can agree whether your project requires language review, comparison with the source, fact-checking or a combination.

What should I include in a project enquiry?

A short brief, a sample or link, the approximate scope and your preferred deadline are a useful starting point. Let me know who the content is for and where it will appear. I welcome enquiries about individual projects, ongoing collaboration and relevant roles, including AI, marketing and video.
