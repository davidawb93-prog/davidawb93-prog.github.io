Norwegian localisation is at the heart of what I do. I also help teams evaluate AI, shape marketing content and tell their stories on screen.

01My core speciality

Norwegian localisation

Content should sound as if it was written for your audience from the start. I translate, refine and quality-check Norwegian with close attention to meaning, tone and the details that make a brand recognisable.

  • English-to-Norwegian translation and cultural adaptation
  • Machine translation post-editing, proofreading and linguistic QA
  • Terminology, style-guide consistency and transcription

Current work includes Norwegian MTPE and linguistic QA for Acclaro. I also offer transcription and subtitling.

02Human judgement for AI

AI training & evaluation

I bring a language specialist's precision to AI training: testing how models reason, checking what they say and giving structured feedback that makes the quality requirements clear.

  • LLM response evaluation, fact-checking and rubric writing
  • Multi-turn, agentic tasks in Norwegian Bokmål
  • Review, trainer coaching and annotation quality
03Words meet visuals

Marketing & creative content
